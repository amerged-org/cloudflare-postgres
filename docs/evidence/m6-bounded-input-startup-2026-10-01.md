pre-birth quota proof, restart fencing or provider runtime is activated here.

while private expected/key files are absent. It pins original readonly input and
writable IPC roots, distinct device/inode and mount identities, ownership/mode,
and private children as they appear. Nested/aliased roots, symlinks and replaced
custody are refused. Final readiness rereads actual boot time, cancellation and

descriptors remain held through supervision. The running supervisor enforces
expiry without continually rechecking root identity after startup.
